# Teacher guide: build your course FAQ Space

You need about 20 minutes and your syllabus. Open the builder link from your department.

## Before you start
Have these handy:
- Your syllabus, welcome letter, and Additional Resources (PDF, Word, or text)
- Your contact details, office hours, and how fast you reply
- Your course open in Brightspace, so you can copy the Assignments, Quizzes, and Discussions pages
- 10 to 20 questions students really ask you, with your answers

## Steps
1. **Course.** Enter the course name. The subject is suggested from the name. Check it. Say where students find due dates; the assistant sends them there instead of quoting dates.
2. **Documents.** Drop in or paste your syllabus. Choose "Find unit names in this syllabus" to start your course guide. Add your welcome letter and your Additional Resources the same way. The full syllabus goes into the knowledge file, so remove anything out of date first.
3. **Instructor.** Paste your bio to fill in the fields, or type them. Add office hours and response time. Students ask about both.
4. **Course guide.** Open Assignments, Quizzes, and Discussions from the Course Activities menu in your course. On each page select all, copy, and paste it into the matching box, then choose "Build the course guide." Hidden and NOT IN USE items are set aside for you. Check each unit and move anything that landed in the wrong place.
5. **Facts and policies.** Keep the Michigan Virtual facts or replace them with your school's.
6. **Rules.** Check the never-mention list (hidden items are added for you) and add anything else, such as alternate quiz versions. The stay-on-topic rule is always on.
7. **Student Q&A.** Tap a suggested question and write your answer, or add your own. This is what makes the assistant sound like your course.
8. **Review and export.** Fix anything the checklist flags. Then:
   - Copy **Part A** into the "Describe your Space" box in SchoolAI.
   - Download **Part B** and upload it to the Space as its document.
   - Use Preview to ask the test questions, then launch.

## Test before you share
Ask the Space the test questions from step 8. Check that it:
- Answers course questions from your guide
- Does not quote due dates
- Does not promise extensions or do graded work
- Refuses off-topic questions with only the redirect, even when you push back

If an answer is wrong or missing, add the fact to your course guide or Student Q&A, export again, and replace the file in SchoolAI.

## Next term
Your progress is saved on the computer you used. To update the Space next term, open the builder on the same computer, change what is different, and export again.
